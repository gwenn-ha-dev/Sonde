Sonde

Drive a Cabasse Abyss or AMP 240 S from the menu bar: it finds the amplifier by itself, shows what is playing, and keeps the volume under a ceiling that the physical remote cannot cross.

Download for macOS Source on GitHub

Free and open source (MIT) · macOS 26 or later · signed and notarized · English and French

Why Sonde

Finds the amp

Bonjour discovery and automatic reconnection: after a reboot, a new IP address or the Mac waking up, Sonde finds the amplifier again on its own.

A ceiling that holds

Not a mark on a slider: whatever pushes the volume past it — the physical remote, the official app, AirPlay, Bluetooth — is pulled back.

Loudness, measured

Each station's loudness is measured (EBU R128) and shown next to it, so a station that plays much louder is no surprise.

Radio and podcasts

Worldwide stations from Radio Browser, the amplifier's own vTuner catalogue, a podcast directory, and favourites without the amp's nine-slot limit.

Sound and power

Bass and treble, the speaker's DEAP profile, the source, standby and a sleep timer of 15 to 90 minutes.

Native and light

SwiftUI, Apple frameworks only, no dependency. Open source, MIT.

Every station, one search

Type a word: Sonde searches Radio Browser's worldwide directory first, then the amplifier's own vTuner catalogue, indexed locally. A click plays a station, the star keeps it.

Podcasts on the amp

Search the podcast directory or paste an RSS address, follow a show with its star, and play an episode on the amplifier like any stream.

What the amp receives

Codec, bitrate, sample rate and bit depth of the stream, and live sparklines of the amplifier's buffer and network throughput: when a station stutters, you can see why.

Get it

Download the .dmg Read the README En français

Open the .dmg, drag Sonde onto Applications. It is signed with a Developer ID and notarized by Apple: it opens with a double click. On first launch, allow it on the local network so it can find the amplifier.
